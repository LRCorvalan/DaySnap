Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

DaySnap does not collect your data. Your videos are yours, and we never receive them.

What we collect

Nothing. DaySnap has no accounts, and CorvalanTech does not collect, receive, store, or sell any information about you or your videos.

Your videos

The clips you record are kept on your iPhone. We have no access to them. You can delete any clip in the app. Deleting the app removes the clips stored in it.

If you back up your iPhone, Apple may include app data in that backup. Those backups are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

DaySnap asks only for what it needs to work on your iPhone. You can change these at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Camera and microphone: to record your clips.
  • Notifications: to remind you to record, if you turn reminders on.

Sharing

A clip leaves DaySnap only when you choose to share it. The app or person you share with handles it under their own policies.

Analytics, ads, and third parties

DaySnap has no ads and no tracking, and we do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any.

Children

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
