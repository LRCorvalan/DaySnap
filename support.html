How do I record a clip?

Tap the camera icon at the top of the app, then tap the record button. Use the button in the top corner to flip the camera.

How do I find an old clip?

Scroll through your list of days, or tap the calendar icon to see a whole month at once.

How do I share or delete a clip?

Open the clip, then tap the share or trash button at the top of the screen.

Where are my videos stored?

On your iPhone. If you delete the app, the clips stored in it are removed too. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

What should I include in my email?

Tell us what happened, your iPhone model, and your iOS version. Screenshots help.
